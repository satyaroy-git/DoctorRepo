Professional Profile

Dr. Prof. Sandeep Kumar Panigrahi is a highly qualified Medicine Specialist (MD) and Professor at Siksha 'O' Anusandhan (SOA) University, Bhubaneswar. With a distinguished career spanning clinical practice, academic teaching, and public health research, he brings evidence-based medicine to everyday patient care.

He has previously served as a Programme Coordinator in Community-Based Child Health as a deputee of UNICEF, contributing to national health survey programmes and infectious disease surveillance initiatives across India.
